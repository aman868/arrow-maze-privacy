Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 09, 2026

Gridline Studio ("we", "our", or "us") developed Arrow Maze: Brain Puzzle as a free-to-play mobile game (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

1. Information Collection and Use

Arrow Maze: Brain Puzzle does not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts). The Game is fully playable offline, and your game progress (such as completed levels and scores) is stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support the development and maintenance of the Game, we use trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify your device. These third-party services include:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general IP-derived coarse location, and ad interaction data to provide relevant ads and combat fraud. For more details, visit Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads"). All financial transactions are handled securely by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or process any payment or credit card details.
  • Google Play Games Services: Used optionally to record achievements.

3. Consent and Advertising Preferences

Where required by applicable law (such as the European Economic Area / UK General Data Protection Regulation and US State Privacy Laws), the Game presents a consent dialog via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can manage or revoke your consent preferences at any time through the in-game Settings menu.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is designed for a general audience (recommended ages 13 and older) and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through a third-party service, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

5. Security

We value your trust. Any data transmitted by third-party SDKs (such as AdMob or Google Play) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/SSL).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
